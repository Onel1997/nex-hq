# Product Vision

NexHQ is a private-owner operating system that converts governed brand truth into reviewable commercial production. It must preserve the authority and provenance of each source domain rather than asking one AI prompt to invent all truth at once.

## Production Domain Model

- **ARTWORK — Design Studio:** a reusable approved visual file with immutable version/checksum and production representations. It exists before and independently of a Product.
- **PRODUCT — Product Library / Shopify:** garment identity, variant, color, material, construction, availability, and visual/surface references. Shopify is authoritative only for verified live catalog truth; manual profiles support samples and future products.
- **PERSONA — Persona Studio:** the approved Brand Model and identity/rights authority — WHO appears in the visual.
- **IMAGE PRODUCTION — Image Studio:** a composition/project boundary that freezes one Artwork + one Product/variant + one Brand Model + one PrintSurface + one shot and produces one reviewable asset.

## Artwork Fidelity Product Promise

Approved Artwork must not be generatively rewritten. The production target is a two-stage flow: generate a clean Persona/Product base, then deterministically apply the original checksummed Artwork pixels using calibrated geometry, clipping, alpha blending, and physically motivated integration. Human approval remains required for final output.

See [Deterministic Mockup Pipeline](./studios/DETERMINISTIC_MOCKUP_PIPELINE.md) and [Product Library](./studios/PRODUCT_LIBRARY.md).

## 2026-08-17 Runtime Milestone

The deterministic product direction now has an implemented no-provider proof: the owner can explicitly bind Artwork, exact Shopify Product/variant, Brand Model, one shot, frozen Product references, and a versioned human-defined PrintSurface; Prepare/Estimate/Confirm freezes that V2 contract; a local synthetic base exercises the durable Stage A seam; Stage B applies the exact checksummed Artwork; and final output requires human checklist review. This does not authorize or validate a real provider-backed base call.

## Owner experience language — 2026-08-17

NexHQ's owner interface is German-first and uses one shared dark, luminous-blue operating-system language. Architecture remains strict underneath, while normal screens expose the next owner decision rather than raw IDs, enums or provider diagnostics. `Artwork`, `Product`, `Persona` and `Image Production` remain visibly separate domains.

## Product knowledge layer — 2026-08-17

Product Library is the canonical bridge between catalog truth and image-production garment fidelity. A Product may be Shopify-live or an owner-managed sample/future profile; both can accumulate explicit material, GSM, fit, construction, reference and placement knowledge without containing Artwork or Persona. Unknown data remains unknown.

## Video production direction — 2026-08-18

Video should begin from an approved deterministic Image asset whenever production continuity matters. Persona Video approval remains independent. Product knowledge and Artwork authority remain separate frozen inputs. Motion/camera are structured direction, not a free-form prompt substitute. One confirmation creates one Video. Exact moving Artwork preservation is a future tracking/compositing capability, not a current provider promise.

## Content Packs and supplier-aware Product truth (2026-08-19)

NexHQ treats Content Packs as owner planning tools, not batch generators. The five-shot Basis-Pack is the reusable starting point; Winning Design Expansion is a larger manual selection surface for a proven Artwork. Every output retains independent cost confirmation, execution, and review.

Supplier/source knowledge belongs to Product Intelligence. MarketPrint, Brandsky, Brandcanyon, another source, or unknown may be represented only from explicit Shopify metadata or a durable owner confirmation. Product-title inference is prohibited.

## Semantic apparel placement (2026-08-19)

The owner selects production intent in apparel language: **Vorne**, **Hinten**, or a truthful **Beidseitig** plan, followed by compatible placements such as **Brust links**, **Großer Frontprint**, or **Großer Backprint**. These labels never replace production authority. A single generated asset still binds one exact Product/variant, one shot, and one versioned geometric `PrintSurface`. `Beidseitig` means two separately prepared assets until a future, explicitly versioned multi-view production contract exists.

## Reusable Product geometry (2026-08-19)

Print placement geometry describes the physical Product, not the Artwork. The owner calibrates a Product surface once and may reuse it across Artworks. Reuse across design-named Shopify listings is allowed only after explicit evidence that they share the same physical blank. Variant inheritance is explicit; unsafe equivalence remains unknown. A production-only fine adjustment is frozen with one job and never silently edits the canonical Product surface.

`Beidseitig` remains a truthful plan for two independently prepared and reviewed assets: one front and one back. Selecting the plan never creates or confirms either job automatically.

## Social Content as the creative expansion engine (2026-08-20)

NexHQ distinguishes two still-image intentions. **Shopify Mockups** are few, consistent and product-first—normally a main image plus one or two supporting views. **Social Content** is varied but controlled: the same approved Artwork, exact Product and selected Markenmodel can be planned across studio, street, parking, stadium, interior, rack, prop, flatlay, detail and campaign executions without changing source truth.

Variation is structured across shot, scene, location, lighting, camera, composition, presentation, mood and channel intent. The owner selects one execution at a time. Suggestions may help avoid a repetitive feed, but they neither randomize production truth nor generate a batch.

## Frictionless routine Product placement (2026-08-20)

Owners choose **Druckseite** and an apparel placement such as **Großer Frontprint**; they do not calibrate four corners during ordinary content creation. NexHQ first reuses exact owner/Product geometry, then verified physical-family geometry, and finally a conservative versioned NexHQ Product-family template for known standard garments. This is still Product knowledge—not Artwork knowledge—and unknown/custom Products fail closed into advanced Product setup rather than receiving fabricated geometry.

The Content Pack selection is also the visible shot step. Removing a duplicate phase does not remove shot authority: one selected shot remains one fingerprinted Prepare, one job, and one asset.

## Complex internals, simple owner flow (2026-08-20)

Daily Image production should feel like a sequence of commercial and creative choices—not infrastructure administration. The owner chooses the approved Artwork, exact Product/color/size, eligible Markenmodel, output goal, one content style, one preset, side, and placement. NexHQ derives and validates Product geometry, provider-neutral direction, cost binding, lineage, and recovery behind that flow. Technical details appear only when requested or when a real blocker requires action.

Shopify Mockups intentionally offer a few consistent choices. Social Content remains the broad creative path, with detailed scene/light/camera controls hidden under **Stil anpassen**. One explicit approval maps to the full durable review record without requiring the owner to tick six redundant boxes.

## Product Family is the physical owner concept (2026-08-20)

**Shopify = commerce truth. Product Family = the owner's physical garment concept. Blank Product references = visual garment truth. MarketPrint green-overlay images = calibration truth. Artwork = the only design truth.** A family such as Vacancy T-Shirt owns colors and one reusable Front/Back printable area; colors supply their own blank Product images without repeating calibration. Shopify Products and variants may map in the background without becoming the primary selection language.

Artwork placement uses only centre-safe auto-fit, uniform scale and translation inside the saved rectangle. It never changes internal proportions. Social and Shopify production continue through the same single-asset deterministic/fabric-aware pipeline after this simpler Product choice.
